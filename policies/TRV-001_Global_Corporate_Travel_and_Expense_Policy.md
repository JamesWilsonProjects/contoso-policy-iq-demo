# POLICY DOCUMENT: TRV-001
**Title:** Global Corporate Travel, Lodging, and Expense Reimbursement Policy  
**Document ID:** TRV-001  
**Category:** Travel & Expense  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Last Reviewed:** October 15, 2026  
**Policy Owner:** Corporate Finance & Travel Risk Management  
**Applies To:** All Employees and Authorized Travelers of Contoso Enterprises Inc.

---

## 1. Purpose and Booking Mandate
This policy governs all domestic and international business travel, lodging, meals, ground transportation, and expense reporting for Contoso Enterprises Inc. ("Contoso").
- **Mandatory Concur Booking Rule:** All airfare, rail, hotel lodging, and rental cars **must** be booked through the **Contoso Concur Travel Portal (`travel.contoso.internal`)** using the employee's **Contoso Corporate Visa Card**. Booking via consumer travel websites (Expedia, Airbnb, VRBO, Booking.com, or personal credit cards) is **prohibited** and will not be reimbursed without VP Finance exception approval.
- **High-Risk & Fraud-Sensitive City Addenda:** Travel to designated high-risk audit jurisdictions—specifically **Las Vegas, NV (Policy TRV-002)** and **New York City, NY (Policy TRV-003)**—is subject to stricter pre-approval workflows, lodging caps, and fraud-prevention controls that supersede the general terms of this policy where noted.

## 2. Air Travel Booking Windows and Cabin Classes
1. **Advance Booking Requirement:**
   - **Domestic Air Travel:** Must be booked at least **14 calendar days** prior to departure.
   - **International Air Travel:** Must be booked at least **21 calendar days** prior to departure.
   - Bookings made within **7 calendar days** of departure require automated **Department Director pre-approval** in Concur.
2. **Permitted Cabin Class by Flight Duration:**
   - **Flights under 6 hours (scheduled single segment):** **Economy / Main Cabin only.**
   - **Flights between 6 and 8 hours:** **Premium Economy** is permitted.
   - **Continuous flight segments exceeding 8 hours (International Transoceanic):** **Business Class** is permitted ONLY with prior written approval from a **Senior Vice President (SVP)** or C-Suite Officer. First Class is never reimbursable.

## 3. Standard Hotel & Lodging Caps
For standard domestic U.S. cities (excluding Tier-1 or High-Risk Addendum cities covered by **TRV-002** and **TRV-003**):
- **Standard Nightly Room Rate Cap:** **$225.00 USD per night** (before state/local occupancy taxes and mandatory resort fees).
- **Short-Stay Home Rentals:** Airbnb, VRBO, and private residential rentals are **strictly prohibited** due to corporate duty-of-care and physical security requirements.
- **Hotel Folio Requirement:** Regardless of amount, **every hotel stay requires a zero-balance itemized hotel folio** uploaded to Concur showing nightly room charges, taxes, and any room service or incidental charges itemized line-by-line.

## 4. Meals, Per Diem, and Client Entertainment
1. **Individual Employee Travel Meals (Actuals capped at $75.00 USD/day):**
   - Contoso reimburses **actual reasonable meal expenses** (not a flat cash payout) up to a combined daily cap of **$75.00 USD per calendar day** (including tax and up to **20% tip**), broken down as guideline targets of **$18 Breakfast / $22 Lunch / $35 Dinner**.
   - **Alcohol Policy for Individual Meals:** Alcohol is **not reimbursable** on individual employee travel meals. Only up to **1 alcoholic beverage per person** is permitted during an external **Client Entertainment Dinner** where a prospective or active customer is present.
2. **Group Employee Meals (Who Pays Rule):** When two or more Contoso employees dine together, the **most senior employee (highest job level) present must pay the bill** on their Corporate Visa Card and list all attendees' full names and titles in Concur.

## 5. Ground Transportation and Mileage
- **Personal Vehicle Mileage:** Reimbursed at the IRS standard mileage rate of **$0.70 USD per mile** (minus the employee's normal daily round-trip commute mileage), plus actual tolls and airport economy parking (capped at **$28.00/day**).
- **Rental Cars:** Mid-size or compact sedan only. Declining the rental agency's Collision Damage Waiver (CDW/LDW) is mandatory for domestic U.S. rentals because coverage is included under the Contoso Corporate Visa program.Pre-paid fuel packages are non-reimbursable.

## 6. Receipt Thresholds and Expense Submission Deadlines
1. **Itemized Receipt Threshold ($25.00 Rule):** An itemized merchant receipt (showing line items purchased, date, merchant name, and payment method—not just a credit card signature slip) is required for:
   - **Any single expense of $25.00 USD or more**, AND
   - **All hotel folios, airfare, rental cars, and any expense incurred in Las Vegas (TRV-002) or New York City (TRV-003)** regardless of dollar amount.
2. **Submission Deadline (30-Day Rule):** Expense reports must be submitted in Concur within **30 calendar days** of trip completion. Expenses submitted more than **60 calendar days** after the transaction date are classified as taxable income to the employee; expenses submitted after **90 calendar days** are **forfeited and non-reimbursable**.
