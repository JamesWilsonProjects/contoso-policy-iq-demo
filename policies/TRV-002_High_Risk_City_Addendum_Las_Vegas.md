**Last Reviewed:** October 15, 2026

## 1. Purpose and High-Risk Fraud Designation
Due to elevated historical audit findings involving disguised entertainment charges, resort-bundled gaming/spa billings, cash-advance abuse, and weekend personal extensions, **Las Vegas, Nevada (Clark County)** is classified as a **Tier-A High-Risk Fraud & Compliance Jurisdiction**. Every expense report associated with Las Vegas travel undergoes **100% automated and manual line-item audit** by the Corporate Fraud Prevention Unit (`fraud-audit@contoso.internal`) prior to reimbursement.

## 2. Mandatory Dual Pre-Approval Workflow (14-Day Rule)
Unlike standard domestic travel under **TRV-001**, no employee may book or travel to Las Vegas without completing the **High-Risk Travel Authorization (Form TRV-LV1)** in Concur at least **14 calendar days prior to departure**:
1. **Approver 1 — Department Vice President (VP):** Must certify the business necessity of attending the Las Vegas trade show, convention, or customer meeting.
2. **Approver 2 — Corporate Travel Risk & Audit Desk:** Verifies that the employee is registered as an official speaker, booth staffer, or scheduled customer meeting participant.
3. **Proof of Event Registration:** Employees must attach their **official conference badge confirmation or customer meeting agenda** to the Concur pre-approval request before airfare or hotel booking is unlocked.

## 3. Las Vegas Lodging Controls and Nightly Caps
1. **Approved Hotel Properties Only:** Employees must stay at a **Concur Preferred Non-Gaming Business Hotel** (e.g., Vdara, Waldorf Astoria Las Vegas, Four Seasons Las Vegas, Marriott Convention Center, or Hilton Grand Vacations) unless the official conference headquarters requires a Strip resort property.
2. **Split Nightly Rate Caps (Las Vegas Specific):**
   - **Standard Weekday Rate (Sunday – Thursday nights):** Capped at **$185.00 USD per night** (before tax and mandatory resort fee; mandatory resort fee is capped at **$45.00/night**).
   - **Tier-1 Major Convention Rate Exception** (e.g., CES, AWS re:Invent, Black Hat, HIMSS, NAB): Capped at **$250.00 USD per night** (before tax and resort fee) when booked through the official conference housing block.
   - **Weekend Nights (Friday & Saturday nights):** **Strictly Non-Reimbursable** unless the official conference program or customer engagement requires formal work duties after 5:00 PM on Friday or on Saturday, and is **explicitly pre-approved in writing by a Senior Vice President (SVP)**.

## 4. Strict Anti-Fraud Expense Rules for Las Vegas
1. **Zero-Dollar ($0.01+) Itemized Receipt Mandate:** The standard $25.00 receipt threshold in **TRV-001** is **suspended** in Las Vegas. **Every single transaction of $0.01 or more** requires a full, itemized merchant receipt showing each food/beverage item ordered. Credit card summary slips or "Room Charge - Restaurant" lines on a hotel folio without the underlying itemized restaurant check are **automatically rejected**.
2. **Prohibited Venues and Charges (Zero Tolerance):**
   - **Casino Floor, Sportsbook, Nightclub, Dayclub, Pool Cabana, and Lounge Bills:** Any food, beverage, or entertainment charge originating from a nightclub, dayclub, pool club, sportsbook, or casino-floor bar is **100% non-reimbursable**, even if a client is present.
   - **Spa, Golf, and Show Tickets:** Strictly prohibited, including for client entertainment.
   - **Cash Advances and ATM Withdrawals:** ATM access and cash advances on the Contoso Corporate Visa Card are **hard-blocked across all Clark County, NV merchant codes**. Employees may not submit cash-paid expenses (other than customary $2–$5 baggage/housekeeping tips up to $10/day total) in Las Vegas.
3. **Daily Meal Cap in Las Vegas:** Individual daily meals remain capped at **$75.00 USD per day** (**$18 Breakfast / $22 Lunch / $35 Dinner**). **Zero alcohol** may be expensed on individual or internal employee group meals.
4. **Client Entertainment in Las Vegas:** Any client meal in Las Vegas requires:
   - Prior written approval from the **Department VP** before the meal occurs;
   - Maximum spend of **$110.00 USD per person** (inclusive of food, max 1 alcoholic beverage, tax, and tip); and
   - Full name, company name, and job title of every external attendee logged in Concur within **48 hours** of the meal.

## 5. Audit Violations and Disciplinary Consequences
Attempting to disguise personal entertainment, gaming, alcohol, nightclub charges, or unauthorized weekend stays as business meals or hotel charges in Las Vegas constitutes **Corporate Expense Fraud**. First-offense violations result in mandatory repayment via payroll deduction, permanent revocation of the Corporate Visa Card, and disciplinary action up to and including **immediate termination of employment** under Policy **HR-001**.
