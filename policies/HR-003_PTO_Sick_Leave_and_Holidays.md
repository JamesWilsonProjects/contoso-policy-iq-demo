## 1. Purpose
This policy defines accrual rates, rollover caps, approval rules, and payout terms for Vacation/Paid Time Off (PTO), Paid Sick and Safe Leave, Company Holidays, Bereavement Leave, and Jury/Civic Duty at Contoso Enterprises Inc. ("Contoso"). Part-time employees scheduled between 20 and 39 hours per week accrue benefits on a pro-rata basis.

## 2. Paid Time Off (Vacation) Accrual Tiers
Full-time employees (both Exempt and Non-Exempt) accrue PTO semi-monthly on each regular payday according to their continuous years of service:

| Tenure Tier (Continuous Service) | Annual PTO Entitlement | Semi-Monthly Accrual Rate (24 pay periods/yr) | Maximum Accrual Cap (Ceiling) | Maximum Year-End Rollover |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier A: 0 to < 3 Years** | **15 days** (120 hours) | 5.00 hours per pay period | **180 hours** (22.5 days) | **40 hours** (5 days) |
| **Tier B: 3 to < 7 Years** | **20 days** (160 hours) | 6.67 hours per pay period | **240 hours** (30.0 days) | **40 hours** (5 days) |
| **Tier C: 7+ Years** | **25 days** (200 hours) | 8.33 hours per pay period | **300 hours** (37.5 days) | **40 hours** (5 days) |

### 2.1 Rollover and Forfeiture Rules
- **Annual Rollover Cap:** Up to **40 hours (5 days)** of unused accrued PTO may be carried over from one calendar year into the next on **January 1**.
- **Use-It-or-Lose-It Window:** Any accrued PTO exceeding 40 hours on December 31 is forfeited unless the employee resides in a jurisdiction that prohibits forfeiture of earned vacation (e.g., California, Colorado, Montana, Nebraska), where instead the **Maximum Accrual Cap (1.5x annual entitlement)** applies and accrual pauses once the cap is reached until hours are used.
- **Negative PTO Balance:** Employees may borrow up to **-16 hours (-2 days)** of unaccrued PTO with written manager approval. Borrowing beyond -16 hours is prohibited.

### 2.2 Notice and Approval Requirements for PTO
- **1 to 4 Consecutive Days:** Requires manager request in Workday at least **5 business days** in advance.
- **5 to 10 Consecutive Days:** Requires manager request in Workday at least **14 calendar days** in advance.
- **More than 10 Consecutive Business Days (2+ Weeks):** Requires written approval from both the direct manager and the **Department Director or VP** at least **30 calendar days** in advance.

### 2.3 Separation Payout
Upon voluntary resignation or involuntary termination, Contoso pays out **100% of unused, accrued PTO** at the employee's final base hourly rate on their final paycheck.

## 3. Paid Sick and Safe Leave
1. **Annual Front-Loaded Grant:** Every January 1 (or upon hire), full-time employees receive **10 days (80 hours)** of Paid Sick Leave front-loaded into their Workday balance.
2. **Permitted Uses:** Personal illness, medical/dental appointments, mental health recovery days, caring for an ill immediate family member, or addressing safety/relocation needs resulting from domestic violence or stalking.
3. **Doctor's Note Requirement:** If an employee uses **more than 3 consecutive scheduled workdays** of Paid Sick Leave, the employee must provide a healthcare provider's work-release note to HR Leave Administration (`leaves@contoso.internal`) and may be transitioned to FMLA Leave under Policy **LEAVE-001**.
4. **No Rollover or Payout:** Unused Paid Sick Leave does **not** carry over at year-end (resetting to 80 hours on January 1) and is **never paid out** upon termination of employment.

## 4. Company Holidays and Floating Holidays
Contoso observes **11 Paid Corporate Holidays** per calendar year (New Year's Day, MLK Jr. Day, Presidents' Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, Day after Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, and Christmas Day) plus **2 Paid Floating Holidays** (16 hours) for cultural, religious, or personal observance. Floating Holidays must be used by **December 31** or are forfeited without payout.

## 5. Bereavement Leave
Employees are eligible for paid Bereavement Leave upon the death of a family member or household member:
- **Tier 1 Immediate Family** (Spouse, domestic partner, child, stepchild, parent, stepparent, sibling, or pregnancy loss / stillbirth): Up to **10 paid business days**.
- **Tier 2 Extended Family** (Grandparent, grandchild, parent-in-law, brother/sister-in-law, aunt, uncle, or legal guardian): Up to **5 paid business days**.
- **Domestic Travel Extension:** Up to **2 additional unpaid or PTO-funded travel days** are automatically approved if the funeral or memorial service requires travel exceeding **500 miles** one-way.

## 6. Jury Duty and Civic Leave
- **Jury Duty & Subpoenaed Witness Leave:** Contoso provides up to **10 business days (80 hours)** of fully paid Jury Duty Leave per calendar year. Employees must upload the official court summons and attendance certificate to Workday.
- **Voting Leave:** Employees who do not have at least 3 consecutive non-working hours while polls are open may take up to **2 hours of paid time off** on Election Day to vote.
