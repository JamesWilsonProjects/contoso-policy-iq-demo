## 1. Purpose and Eligibility
Contoso Enterprises Inc. ("Contoso") invests in continuous employee growth through two separate funding programs:
1. **Academic Tuition Assistance Program** (for accredited college/university degree programs), and
2. **Professional Certification & Technical Training Budget** (for industry exams, bootcamps, and workshops).

### 1.1 Eligibility Requirements
To qualify for either program, an employee must:
1. Be a **Regular Full-Time Employee** (scheduled 40 hours/week; contractors, interns, and part-time employees are not eligible).
2. Have completed at least **6 months (180 calendar days)** of continuous service prior to the course or exam start date.
3. Hold a most recent performance rating of **3 (Meets Expectations) or higher** and not be on an active PIP or formal disciplinary warning (per Policy **HR-004**).

## 2. Academic Tuition Assistance Program ($5,250 Annual Cap)
1. **Annual IRS Tax-Free Limit:** Contoso reimburses up to **$5,250.00 USD per calendar year** for eligible tuition, mandatory laboratory fees, and required textbooks at an accredited college or university (Associate, Bachelor's, Master's, MBA, or Doctoral degree directly related to Contoso's business operations).
   - Non-eligible expenses include: parking permits, campus health fees, meal plans, late registration fees, laptop hardware, and travel to campus.
2. **Pre-Approval Window:** Employees must submit Form **LND-101 (Tuition Pre-Approval)** in Workday and obtain both Direct Manager and L&D approval **at least 14 calendar days BEFORE the academic term starts**. Retroactive requests submitted after a class has started are automatically denied.
3. **Grade-Based Reimbursement Schedule:** Reimbursement is paid only after the term ends and the employee uploads an official transcript and itemized bursar receipt within **60 calendar days** of course completion:

| Final Course Grade (or Equivalent) | Reimbursement Percentage |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Grade "A", "A-", "B+", "B"** (or "Pass" in a mandatory Pass/Fail course) | **100%** of eligible costs (up to the $5,250 annual cap) |
| **Grade "B-" or "C+" / "C"** | **50%** of eligible costs |
| **Grade "C-", "D", "F", "Incomplete", or "Withdrawn"** | **0%** (No reimbursement) |

## 3. Tuition Clawback / Repayment Agreement (12-Month Retention Rule)
Before receiving academic tuition reimbursement, the employee must sign the **Tuition Repayment Agreement**:
- **12-Month Commitment:** If an employee voluntarily resigns or is terminated for cause within **12 months (365 days)** of the date a tuition reimbursement payment is disbursed, the employee must repay Contoso on a pro-rata schedule:
  - **Departure within 0 to 6 months of disbursement:** **100% repayment** required.
  - **Departure within 6 to 12 months of disbursement:** **50% repayment** required.
  - **Departure after 12 months (or involuntary layoff / reduction in force):** **0% repayment** (fully vested).

## 4. Professional Certifications and Technical Training ($2,000 Annual Allowance)
Separate from academic degree tuition, each eligible full-time employee has an annual **Professional Development & Certification Allowance of $2,000.00 USD per calendar year**:
1. **Covered Items:** Industry certification exams (e.g., Microsoft Azure, AWS, CISSP, PMP, CPA, SHRM), official exam prep courses, and technical bootcamps.
2. **Exam Attempt Rule:** Contoso reimburses **100% of the exam fee upon a passing score** on the first or second attempt. Failed exam attempts are **not** reimbursed until the employee passes the exam, up to a maximum of one reimbursed exam pass per certification track.
3. **No Clawback on Certifications:** Reimbursements under the $2,000.00 Professional Certification Allowance are **not** subject to the 12-month repayment clawback.
4. **Paid Study Days:** Employees may take up to **2 paid Study/Exam Days (16 hours) per calendar year** with manager approval to prepare for and sit for a job-relevant professional certification exam.
