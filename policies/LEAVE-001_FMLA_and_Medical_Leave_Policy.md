## 1. Purpose and Overview
Contoso Enterprises Inc. ("Contoso") complies with the federal Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA) and applicable state family and medical leave laws. This policy outlines eligibility requirements, job-protected leave durations, medical certification rules, benefit continuation, and coordination with Contoso's Paid Parental Leave and Short-Term Disability (STD) programs.

## 2. FMLA Eligibility Criteria
To qualify for federal FMLA leave, an employee must satisfy **all three** statutory requirements on the date leave begins:
1. **Tenure Requirement:** Have worked for Contoso for at least **12 months** (the 12 months need not be consecutive, provided any break in service was less than 7 years).
2. **Hours Worked Requirement:** Have actually worked at least **1,250 hours** during the **12-month period immediately preceding** the start of the leave (paid PTO, sick days, and holidays do not count toward the 1,250 actual hours worked threshold).
3. **Worksite Requirement:** Work at a location where Contoso employs at least **50 employees within a 75-mile radius** (for Designated Remote employees under Policy **HR-002**, the "worksite" is the corporate office to which they report or receive assignments, not their personal residence).

## 3. Qualifying Reasons and Leave Duration
1. **Standard 12-Week Entitlement:** Eligible employees may take up to **12 workweeks (60 business days / 480 hours)** of job-protected leave in a **rolling 12-month period measured backward** from the date an employee uses any FMLA leave for:
   - The birth of a child and bonding with the newborn within 12 months of birth;
   - The placement of a child with the employee for adoption or foster care within 12 months of placement;
   - Caring for an immediate family member (**spouse, child, or parent** — *note: parents-in-law and siblings are not covered under federal FMLA*) with a serious health condition;
   - A serious health condition that makes the employee unable to perform the essential functions of their job; or
   - Any qualifying exigency arising out of the fact that the employee's spouse, child, or parent is a military member on covered active duty.
2. **Military Caregiver Leave (26-Week Entitlement):** Eligible employees may take up to **26 workweeks** of leave during a single 12-month period to care for a covered servicemember or veteran with a serious injury or illness incurred in the line of duty.

## 4. Coordination with Paid Leave, Short-Term Disability (STD), and PTO
FMLA provides unpaid job protection, but Contoso coordinates paid wage-replacement benefits that run **concurrently** (at the same time) with FMLA leave:

| Leave Scenario | Concurrent Wage Replacement Program | Pay Rate & Duration |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Employee's Own Serious Health Condition / Childbirth Recovery** | **Short-Term Disability (STD)** after a **5-business-day elimination period** (funded by Paid Sick Leave or PTO) | **100% of base pay** for Weeks 2 through 6; **66.67% of base pay** for Weeks 7 through 12. |
| **Paid Parental Bonding Leave** (Birth, Adoption, or Foster Placement — All Parents) | **Contoso Paid Parental Leave** (runs concurrently with FMLA) | **6 workweeks (240 hours) at 100% of base pay** for all eligible birthing and non-birthing parents; remaining 6 weeks of FMLA may be funded with accrued PTO or taken unpaid. |
| **Caring for a Family Member with a Serious Health Condition** | **Paid Caregiver Leave + Accrued Sick/PTO** | **2 workweeks (80 hours) at 100% of base pay** via Contoso Caregiver Leave, followed by optional use of Paid Sick Leave / PTO or unpaid FMLA. |

## 5. Notice, Medical Certification, and Privacy Guardrails
1. **Advance Notice Timelines:**
   - **Foreseeable Leave** (e.g., expected childbirth, planned surgery): The employee must open a leave claim in Workday or with Contoso's third-party leave administrator (**Sedgwick Leave Services**) at least **30 calendar days** in advance.
   - **Unforeseeable / Emergency Leave:** Notice must be provided as soon as practicable, and no later than **2 business days** after learning of the need for leave.
2. **Medical Certification Deadline (15-Calendar-Day Rule):** Once a leave request is opened, the employee must submit completed **Form WH-380-E (Employee)** or **WH-380-F (Family Member)** signed by a licensed healthcare provider directly to Sedgwick within **15 calendar days**. Failure to provide certification within 15 calendar days (unless impracticable) may result in delay or denial of FMLA protection.
3. **Strict Medical Privacy & PII Rule:** Employees must **never** disclose specific medical diagnoses, genetic information, prescriptions, SSNs, or clinical records to their direct manager, peers, or internal AI assistants. All medical documentation must be uploaded exclusively to the encrypted **Sedgwick Leave Portal**.

## 6. Intermittent Leave, Benefits Continuation, and Return to Work
1. **Intermittent or Reduced-Schedule Leave:** Medically necessary intermittent FMLA leave may be taken in increments as small as **15 minutes**. Intermittent leave for **parental bonding** (when not medically necessary) requires direct manager approval and must be taken in minimum blocks of **1 full workweek (5 consecutive days)**.
2. **Health Benefits Continuation:** During approved FMLA leave, Contoso continues the employee's medical, dental, and vision coverage on the same terms as active employment. If the leave is unpaid, the employee must remit their regular employee premium share monthly via direct billing.
3. **Fitness-for-Duty Release:** Before returning from FMLA leave taken for the employee's own serious health condition, the employee must provide a healthcare provider's **Fitness-for-Duty Certificate** at least **2 business days** prior to returning to work.
