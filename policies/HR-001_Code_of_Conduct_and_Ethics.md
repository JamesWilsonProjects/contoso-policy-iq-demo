## 1. Purpose and Scope
This policy establishes the ethical standards, anti-bribery rules, gift acceptance limits, conflict-of-interest disclosures, and outside employment rules for all employees of Contoso Enterprises Inc. ("Contoso"). Every employee must complete the annual Ethics & Compliance certification within 30 calendar days of hire and by December 15 of each calendar year thereafter.

## 2. Gifts, Meals, and Entertainment Acceptance Limits
Employees must never accept or offer gifts, meals, or entertainment that could influence—or appear to influence—objective business judgment.

| Category | Threshold / Cap | Approval & Disclosure Requirement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Promotional Items / Swag** | Up to **$25.00 USD** per item | Permitted without disclosure (e.g., branded pens, notebooks, t-shirts). |
| **Non-Cash Business Gifts** | **$25.01 to $100.00 USD** per vendor per calendar year | Permitted only if logged in the **Ethics Disclosure Portal (`ethics.contoso.internal`)** within **5 business days** of receipt. |
| **High-Value Gifts** | Exceeding **$100.00 USD** | **Strictly Prohibited.** Must be politely declined or surrendered to the Office of Ethics & Legal Compliance for charitable donation or raffle. |
| **Cash and Cash Equivalents** | **$0.00 USD** (Any amount) | **Strictly Prohibited.** Includes gift cards, Visa/Mastercard prepaid cards, cryptocurrency, vouchers, and loans. Zero exceptions. |
| **Vendor-Sponsored Business Meals** | Up to **$125.00 USD** per person | Permitted occasionally if the vendor representative is physically present and active business is discussed. Limit of **2 meals per quarter** from the same vendor. |
| **Sporting, Concert, or Sporting Events** | Any ticket valued over **$100.00 USD** | Requires **written pre-approval** from both the employee's Vice President and the Chief Compliance Officer (CCO) at least **7 business days** prior to the event. |

## 3. Government and Public Sector Officials
Contoso enforces a **Zero-Tolerance Anti-Bribery Rule** for interactions with local, state, federal, or foreign government officials:
1. Employees may **not** provide any gift, meal, coffee, refreshments, or travel accommodation of any dollar value ($0.00 limit) to a public-sector official without prior written clearance from Legal Compliance.
2. Facilitation payments ("grease payments") to expedite routine government actions are strictly prohibited worldwide.

## 4. Conflicts of Interest and Nepotism
A conflict of interest arises when an employee's personal, social, or financial interests interfere with their loyalty to Contoso.
1. **Family & Romantic Relationships (Nepotism Rule):** Employees may not directly or indirectly supervise, evaluate, audit, or influence the compensation or promotion of a relative, spouse, domestic partner, or individual with whom they have a romantic relationship. If such a relationship develops, both employees must notify HR Business Partners (`hrbp@contoso.internal`) within **10 business days** so reporting lines can be reassigned.
2. **Financial Interest in Competitors or Suppliers:** Employees may not hold more than **1% of the publicly traded stock** or any private equity stake exceeding **$10,000 USD** in a direct competitor, vendor, or supplier without written clearance from the Conflict of Interest Committee.

## 5. Outside Employment and Moonlighting
1. **Full-Time Employees (Exempt and Non-Exempt):** Must treat Contoso as their primary employer.
2. **Pre-Approval Requirement:** Before accepting any compensated outside employment, paid advisory board seat, freelance consulting, or independent contractor work, the employee must submit Form **ETH-204 (Outside Activity Request)** and obtain approval from their Department Head and Legal Compliance.
3. **Automatic Disqualification:** Outside employment is automatically denied if it:
   - Involves a competitor, current customer, or active vendor of Contoso;
   - Uses Contoso laptops, software licenses, source code, or proprietary data; or
   - Exceeds **15 hours per week** or occurs during Contoso Core Business Hours (9:00 AM – 3:00 PM local time).

## 6. Whistleblower Protection and Reporting Violations
Employees are required to report known or suspected violations of this policy via the **Contoso Integrity Helpline** at `1-800-555-ETHX` or online at `integrity.contoso.internal`. Anonymous reporting is available 24/7. Contoso enforces a strict **Non-Retaliation Policy**: any manager or employee who retaliates against a good-faith whistleblower is subject to immediate termination of employment.
