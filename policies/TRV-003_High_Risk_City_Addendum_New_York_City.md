## 1. Purpose and High-Risk Audit Designation
New York City (NYC) represents Contoso Enterprises Inc.'s highest-cost domestic travel market and accounts for disproportionate audit exceptions involving inflated luxury hotel bookings, unauthorized private black-car/chauffeured limousine billing, and split-check fine-dining fraud. Consequently, all travel to the five boroughs of NYC (plus Jersey City and Hoboken, NJ) is governed by this mandatory addendum (`TRV-003`).

## 2. NYC Pre-Approval Requirements
1. **Stays of 1 to 3 Nights:** Requires standard **Direct Manager + Department Director** approval in Concur at least **10 calendar days** prior to departure.
2. **Extended Stays (More than 3 Nights) or Peak Season (September – December):** Requires **Vice President (VP) written pre-approval** in Concur (Form **TRV-NY1**) before booking.
3. **Same-Day Amtrak Acela Mandate (Northeast Corridor):** Employees traveling to NYC from Boston, MA; Providence, RI; Philadelphia, PA; Wilmington, DE; Baltimore, MD; or Washington, D.C. **must travel via Amtrak Northeast Regional or Acela Business Class** rather than commercial flights, and are expected to return same-day unless consecutive morning/evening client commitments are documented.

## 3. NYC Lodging Caps and Approved Zones
Because standard domestic hotel caps ($225/night in **TRV-001**) are insufficient for Manhattan, Contoso establishes the following strict NYC lodging tiers:

| **Zone 1: Manhattan (Below 96th Street)** | **$375.00 USD per night** (Peak Q4 Sept 15 – Dec 15 cap: **$425.00 USD/night** with VP approval) | Must use a **Contoso Negotiated Rate Hotel** in Concur. Luxury 5-star properties (e.g., The Plaza, St. Regis, Four Seasons, Aman, Mandarin Oriental, Ritz-Carlton) are **hard-blocked**. |

## 4. NYC Ground Transportation & Anti-Fraud Controls
Audit reviews have identified widespread abuse of hourly black-car services and luxury SUVs in NYC. The following rules are strictly enforced:
1. **Prohibition on Private Black Cars, Limousines, and Hourly Chauffeurs:** Booking private town cars, executive sedans, Blade helicopters, or Uber Black / Uber Black SUV / Lyft Lux is **strictly prohibited and 100% non-reimbursable**.
2. **Airport Transfers (JFK, LGA, EWR to Manhattan):**
   - Employees are strongly encouraged to use **MTA Subway, Long Island Rail Road (LIRR), AirTrain, or NJ Transit** (100% reimbursable).
   - Standard yellow taxi or standard **UberX / Lyft Standard** from JFK, LGA, or EWR to Manhattan is permitted up to a hard cap of **$95.00 USD one-way** (including tolls and up to 18% tip). Any airport transfer exceeding $95.00 requires a screenshot showing severe transit disruption and Director sign-off.
3. **Intra-City Manhattan Travel (Subway First Rule):**
   - For travel within Manhattan between 6:00 AM and 9:00 PM, employees must use the **MTA Subway or Bus** (`OMNY` tap-to-pay).
   - Rideshare (`UberX` / `Lyft Standard`) or yellow cabs within Manhattan are reimbursable **only**:
     - After **9:00 PM** for personal safety;
     - During severe inclement weather; or
     - When transporting heavy client presentation equipment (>25 lbs).
   - **Mandatory GPS Route Receipt:** Every rideshare receipt submitted in NYC must include the **full PDF/email receipt showing exact pickup address, drop-off address, timestamp, and route map**.

## 5. NYC Meal Per Diem and Client Entertainment Controls
1. **Individual Employee Daily Meal Cap (NYC Tier-1 Rate):** Increased from the standard $75/day to **$95.00 USD per calendar day** (including tax and max 20% tip), with guideline sub-caps of **$22 Breakfast / $28 Lunch / $45 Dinner**.
   - **Zero-Dollar ($0.01+) Itemized Receipt Rule:** Just like Las Vegas (**TRV-002**), **every meal or ground transport expense of $0.01 or more in NYC requires an itemized receipt** showing individual line items.
   - **Split-Check Prohibition:** Employees dining together may **never split a restaurant bill across multiple Corporate Visa Cards** to stay under the $45 dinner cap or avoid VP review. Concur's duplicate-merchant/timestamp AI detector automatically flags split checks within 30 minutes at the same venue as a **Critical Fraud Violation**.
2. **Client Entertainment Meals in NYC:**
   - Capped at **$125.00 USD per person** (inclusive of food, max 1 alcoholic beverage per attendee, tax, and tip).
   - Any client dinner where total bill exceeds **$500.00 USD** or per-person cost exceeds **$125.00 USD** requires **written pre-approval from the Department VP** at least **24 hours prior** to the dinner and full CRM Opportunity ID + attendee title logging in Concur.
