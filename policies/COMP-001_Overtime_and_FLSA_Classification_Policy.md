## 1. Purpose and FLSA Classification
Contoso Enterprises Inc. ("Contoso") complies with the federal Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA) and all applicable state and local wage-and-hour laws. Every position at Contoso is classified in Workday as either:
1. **Exempt (Salaried):** Exempt from FLSA overtime pay requirements (e.g., Executive, Administrative, Professional, Computer Employee, and Outside Sales exemptions). Exempt employees receive a fixed bi-weekly salary intended to compensate them for all hours worked and are **not eligible** for overtime pay or compensatory ("comp") time off.
2. **Non-Exempt (Hourly or Salaried Non-Exempt):** Eligible for overtime compensation for all hours worked beyond statutory daily or weekly thresholds.

## 2. Standard Workweek and Overtime Calculation Rates
The official Contoso workweek for payroll calculation begins on **Sunday at 12:00 AM (Midnight)** and ends on **Saturday at 11:59 PM** local time.

| Overtime Tier | Trigger Condition | Overtime Pay Multiplier | Notes & State Exceptions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Standard Weekly Overtime** | Hours physically worked over **40 hours** in a single workweek | **1.5x** regular rate of pay (Time-and-a-Half) | Paid PTO, Paid Sick Leave, Jury Duty, and Holidays do **not** count as "hours physically worked" toward the 40-hour threshold. |
| **California / Daily Overtime Rule** (where applicable by state law) | Hours physically worked over **8 hours up to 12 hours** in a single workday, and the first **8 hours on the 7th consecutive workday** | **1.5x** regular rate of pay | Applies automatically to Non-Exempt employees working in California or other daily-overtime jurisdictions. |
| **Double-Time (2.0x) Overtime** | Hours worked over **12 hours in a single workday**, OR hours worked over **8 hours on the 7th consecutive workday** in a workweek, OR hours worked on an **Official Company Holiday** | **2.0x** regular rate of pay (Double-Time) | Applies company-wide for Company Holiday work and in applicable states for >12 hr/day or 7th-day work. |
| **Maximum Weekly Cap (Safety Limit)** | Total hours worked in a single workweek (Regular + Overtime combined) | **Hard Cap of 60 Hours / Week** | No Non-Exempt employee may work more than **12 hours in a single day** or **60 total hours in a workweek** due to occupational safety rules. |

## 3. Mandatory Written Pre-Approval Rule
1. **Prior Manager Authorization Required:** Non-Exempt employees must obtain **written pre-approval in Workday (or via email/Teams from their direct manager)** **BEFORE** working any overtime hours, starting early, staying late, working through a meal period, or checking email/Teams after hours.
2. **Emergency Exception (>8 Hours Overtime / Week):** A direct manager may approve up to **8 hours of overtime per employee per workweek**. Any overtime exceeding **8 hours in a single workweek** (i.e., >48 total hours) requires prior written approval from the **Department Director or Vice President**.
3. **Prohibition on "Comp Time":** Managers are **strictly prohibited** from offering "comp time" (informal time off in a future workweek in lieu of paying overtime) to Non-Exempt employees.

## 4. Unauthorized Overtime and "Off-the-Clock" Work Prohibition
1. **Strict Prohibition on Off-the-Clock Work:** Non-Exempt employees are strictly prohibited from performing any work "off the clock" (unrecorded), including checking work email or Microsoft Teams on mobile devices outside scheduled shift hours.
2. **Pay Mandate for Unauthorized Overtime:** In accordance with federal law, if a Non-Exempt employee works overtime **without** prior manager approval, **Contoso will still pay the employee at the applicable 1.5x or 2.0x overtime rate** for all time worked.
3. **Disciplinary Action for Unauthorized Overtime:** While unauthorized overtime must always be accurately recorded and paid, working overtime without prior written approval violates this policy:
   - **1st Occurrence:** Documented verbal coaching from the manager.
   - **2nd Occurrence (within 90 days):** Formal Written Warning via Employee Relations.
   - **3rd Occurrence (within 180 days):** Final Written Warning or termination of employment.
   - *Note:* Any manager who instructs, encourages, or knowingly permits a Non-Exempt employee to work off the clock or falsify timecards is subject to **immediate termination of employment**.

## 5. Meal and Rest Break Requirements for Non-Exempt Employees
1. **Paid Rest Breaks:** One paid, duty-free **15-minute rest break** for every **4 hours worked** (or major fraction thereof), taken near the middle of each 4-hour work block.
2. **Unpaid Meal Period:** Any Non-Exempt employee scheduled to work **more than 5 hours** in a day must take an uninterrupted, duty-free, **unpaid 30-minute meal period** before the end of their **5th hour of work** (and a second 30-minute meal period if working more than 10 hours). Employees must clock out and back in via Workday Time Tracking for all meal periods.

## 6. On-Call and Standby Pay
Non-Exempt IT, Security, and Facilities employees assigned to a formal scheduled **On-Call Rotation** (where they must respond to pages within 30 minutes) receive a flat **$50.00 USD per weekday on-call shift** and **$100.00 USD per weekend/holiday 24-hour shift**, plus **1.5x overtime pay** (with a minimum **2-hour call-back guarantee**) for actual time spent resolving incidents.
