## 1. Purpose and Work Arrangement Classifications
Contoso Enterprises Inc. ("Contoso") supports a structured hybrid workplace model designed to balance in-person collaboration with remote productivity. Every employee is assigned one of three official work designations in Workday:

| Work Designation | In-Office Requirement | Workspace Assignment | Eligibility & Approval |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1: On-Site Essential** | **5 days per week** on-site | Dedicated desk, lab, or facility station | Required for hardware engineering, data center operations, facilities, and reception. |
| **Tier 2: Standard Hybrid (Default)** | Minimum **3 days per week** in an assigned Contoso regional hub office | Shared neighborhood hoteling desk | Default classification for all employees living within a **50-mile radius** of a Contoso office. |
| **Tier 3: Designated Remote** | **0 days per week** routine office attendance (quarterly travel may be required) | Home office only | Requires **VP-level approval** plus HR People Operations sign-off, or residence >50 miles from any Contoso office at time of hire. |

## 2. Standard Hybrid Attendance Rules
1. **Anchor Days:** Unless otherwise designated by a department Vice President, **Tuesday and Wednesday** are company-wide Anchor Days. Standard Hybrid employees must attend in-person on both Anchor Days plus at least **one additional day** of their choosing (Monday, Thursday, or Friday), totaling **at least 3 days per week**.
2. **Badge Compliance Tracking:** Building access badge swipes are aggregated monthly at the department level. Falling below an average of **12 in-office days per calendar month** (excluding approved PTO, sick leave, Holidays, or business travel) for **two consecutive months** triggers a formal manager coaching conversation and may disqualify the employee from discretionary bonus eligibility.

## 3. Core Collaboration Hours and Availability
All employees—whether working on-site, hybrid, or remote—must be online, reachable via Microsoft Teams, and available for synchronous meetings during **Core Collaboration Hours: 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM** in the time zone of their assigned primary office (unless on approved leave).
- **Non-Exempt (Hourly) Employees** may **not** check email, respond to Teams messages, or perform any work outside their scheduled shift hours without prior written overtime authorization per Policy **COMP-001**.

## 4. Home Office Equipment Stipend and Internet Reimbursement
1. **One-Time Ergonomic Home Office Stipend:** Newly hired Tier 2 (Standard Hybrid) and Tier 3 (Designated Remote) employees are eligible for a one-time reimbursement of up to **$600.00 USD** for ergonomic home office equipment (monitor, ergonomic chair, standing desk converter, keyboard, webcam).
   - Purchases must be submitted via Concur within **90 calendar days** of hire with itemized receipts.
   - Furniture and equipment purchased under this stipend remain the property of the employee after **12 months of continuous service**; if the employee voluntarily resigns within 12 months, **50% ($300.00 max)** is deducted from final pay where permitted by state law.
2. **Refresh Cycle:** Employees become eligible for a **$300.00 USD** home office refresh stipend once every **36 months** (3 years) of continuous employment.
3. **Monthly Internet & Connectivity Stipend:**
   - **Tier 3 (Designated Remote) Employees:** Receive a taxable **$65.00 USD per month** connectivity stipend automatically via payroll.
   - **Tier 2 (Standard Hybrid) Employees:** Are **not** eligible for monthly home internet reimbursement, except in states where statutory law mandates reimbursement (e.g., California and Illinois, where Tier 2 employees receive **$35.00 USD per month**).

## 5. Temporary "Work from Anywhere" (Domestic & International)
1. **Domestic Work from Elsewhere:** Employees in good performance standing (Rating of 3 or higher) may work temporarily from another U.S. state where Contoso is a registered employer for up to **20 business days per calendar year** with direct manager approval. Working more than **10 consecutive business days** in a single state outside the primary work state requires prior notification to the Corporate Tax & Payroll Team (`taxmobility@contoso.internal`).
2. **International Remote Work Restriction:** Working from outside the United States (including working while on personal vacation abroad) is **strictly prohibited** without prior written approval from **Global Mobility, Legal, and Corporate Information Security** submitted at least **30 calendar days in advance**. Even when approved, international remote work is capped at **10 business days per calendar year** and is completely prohibited in Embargoed or High-Risk Cyber Jurisdictions.

## 6. Information Security and Safe Workspace Requirements
Remote and hybrid employees must:
1. Connect exclusively through the **Contoso GlobalProtect VPN** when accessing internal networks.
2. Never work on confidential documents in public view (e.g., coffee shops, airplanes, hotel lobbies) without a **Contoso-issued 3M privacy screen filter** installed.
3. Never connect Contoso laptops to public Wi-Fi networks that lack WPA2/WPA3 encryption unless routed through a personal cellular hotspot.
