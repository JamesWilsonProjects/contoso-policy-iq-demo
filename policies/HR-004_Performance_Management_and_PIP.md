## 1. Performance Review Cadence
Contoso Enterprises Inc. ("Contoso") operates a bi-annual performance management cycle tracked in Workday:
1. **Mid-Year Check-In (July 1 – July 31):** Mandatory written self-reflection and manager progress review against annual OKRs (Objectives and Key Results). No numerical rating or compensation adjustment occurs at mid-year unless promotional exceptions apply.
2. **Annual Performance & Calibration Cycle (November 1 – December 15):** Comprehensive evaluation of full-year business impact, core competencies, and compliance adherence. Final ratings take effect on **January 1** and govern annual merit increases, bonus multipliers, and equity refresh grants paid on **March 15**.

## 2. Official 5-Point Rating Scale and Bonus Multipliers
All employees receive an overall annual rating on Contoso's 5-point scale during calibration:

| Rating Score | Rating Label | Target Distribution Guideline | Individual Bonus Multiplier | Internal Transfer / Promotion Eligibility |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **5** | **Role Model / Exceptional Impact** | ~5% – 10% | **150%** of target bonus | Eligible immediately; fast-track promotion candidate |
| **4** | **Exceeds Expectations** | ~20% – 25% | **120%** of target bonus | Eligible immediately |
| **3** | **Meets Expectations (Solid Contributor)** | ~55% – 65% | **100%** of target bonus | Eligible after 12 months in current role |
| **2** | **Needs Improvement / Inconsistent** | ~5% – 8% | **0% – 50%** (requires VP approval) | **Ineligible** for internal transfer, promotion, or tuition reimbursement |
| **1** | **Unsatisfactory** | ~1% – 2% | **0%** (No merit or bonus) | **Ineligible**; mandatory formal PIP or separation |

## 3. Internal Mobility and Promotion Eligibility Rules
To apply for an internal transfer or be nominated for a promotion:
1. The employee must have completed at least **12 continuous months** in their current role and level.
2. The employee's most recent annual performance rating must be **3 (Meets Expectations) or higher**.
3. The employee must **not** have an active Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) or a formal written disciplinary warning issued within the preceding **6 months**.

## 4. Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) Process
When an employee's performance falls below acceptable standards (Rating 1 or persistent Rating 2 behaviors), managers must partner with an assigned **Employee Relations Specialist (`er@contoso.internal`)** before initiating a formal PIP.

### 4.1 Prerequisite Informal Coaching
Except in cases of gross misconduct or severe negligence, managers must conduct at least **two documented informal coaching sessions** over a minimum **30-calendar-day period** before placing an employee on a formal PIP.

### 4.2 Formal PIP Structure and Timeline
1. **Standard PIP Duration:** A formal PIP lasts **60 calendar days** (or **30 calendar days** for sales quota roles during ramp or employees in their first 180 days of employment).
2. **Mandatory PIP Elements:** Every PIP document must be approved by Employee Relations and specify:
   - Specific, measurable performance deficiencies with examples;
   - Concrete deliverables and numeric/objective success criteria for Days 15, 30, 45, and 60;
   - **Mandatory Weekly 1:1 Check-Ins:** The manager and employee must meet every week for at least 30 minutes, and the manager must send a written recap in Workday within **24 hours** of each weekly check-in.
3. **PIP Restrictions:** While on an active PIP, an employee is **ineligible** for:
   - Annual merit increases, spot bonuses, or annual performance bonuses;
   - Internal job transfers or promotions;
   - Tuition reimbursement or external conference travel approvals;
   - Temporary "Work from Elsewhere" privileges under Policy **HR-002**.

### 4.3 PIP Outcomes at Day 60
At the conclusion of the 60-day PIP period, the manager and Employee Relations will determine one of three outcomes:
1. **Successful Completion:** The employee meets all PIP objectives and returns to good standing. However, if performance regresses to unsatisfactory levels on the **same core competencies within 180 calendar days (6 months)** of completing the PIP, the employee may be terminated without a second PIP.
2. **One-Time Extension:** If substantial progress is demonstrated or an approved medical/FMLA leave interrupted the timeline, the PIP may be extended once for up to **30 additional calendar days** with HR Director approval.
3. **Termination of Employment:** If the employee fails to meet the stated PIP milestones, employment is terminated for performance.

## 5. Appeal Process
An employee who believes their annual performance rating of **1 or 2** or their placement on a PIP violates company policy or reflects unlawful bias may submit a formal written appeal to Employee Relations within **7 business days** of receiving the rating or PIP document. An independent HR Director outside the employee's reporting chain will review the documentation and issue a final determination within **15 business days**.
